Message Desk privacy

Message Desk is SENT Life Center's internal tool for preparing recorded messages for sentlifecenter.com. Last updated November 8, 2026.

Who can use it

Only members of the SENT Life Center tech team whose Google accounts are on the approved list can sign in.

What we use from your Google account

When you sign in, Message Desk receives your name and email address. They are used only to check that you are on the team list and to keep you signed in. Nothing else from your Google account is read or stored.

Recordings and files

Recordings, notes files, edits and finished videos are stored in SENT Life Center's own Google Drive (sentlifecenter.tech@gmail.com). Finished messages are published to SENT Life Center's Vimeo account and to sentlifecenter.com only when a team member chooses to publish them.

What we don't do

Message Desk does not sell or share data, show ads, or track you across other websites.
